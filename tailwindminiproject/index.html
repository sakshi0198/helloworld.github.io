About Greenwood

A quiet place to discover, learn and connect.

Greenwood Public Library has served the local community since 1983. What started as a small neighbourhood library has grown into a welcoming space for readers of all ages.

Our collection includes thousands of books, journals, digital resources and learning materials. Visitors can also use our free Wi-Fi and dedicated study spaces.

Beyond books, the library hosts workshops, reading groups, children's activities and community events throughout the year.

Whether you need a quiet place to work or simply want to discover your next great read, Greenwood is open to you.
